BUILT FROM EXPERIENCE

I run a food truck.
I also write software.

Running a food truck means doing a lot more than serving food. There's the booking, the event details, the staffing, the tips, and the question at the end of the day: was that event worth it?

I'm building Food Truck Works because I work through those same problems in my own business. My experience as a software engineer gives me a way to turn them into tools that help.

This is where Food Truck Works started: real work, real events, and a belief that the tools we use can fit our day better.

Built at the intersection of code and the service window.
